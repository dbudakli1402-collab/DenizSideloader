# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Essentials)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Deniz Sideloader a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity, experience level, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards

- Be kind, precise, and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of shaming.
- Accept feedback gracefully; focus on the technical merit.
- No harassment, insulting comments, or unwanted attention — in issues,
  PRs, discussions, or DMs related to the project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove
contributors who violate this code.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions)
and in public when representing the project.
